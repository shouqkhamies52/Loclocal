How it works

Three steps to an unforgettable UAE trip

No tour buses. No scripted itineraries. Just you, a local who knows their city, and the UAE the way it really is.

01
Set your budget & interests
Tell us what you love — desert adventures, local food, cultural heritage, family activities. Set your budget. We'll match you with the right local guide.
02
Your guide builds the plan
Your local guide creates a custom itinerary — what to do, where to eat, when to arrive. They pick you up in their car and take you to places only a local knows.
03
Live updates, real experiences
Check real-time crowd levels before you visit. Chat with locals already at the venue. Book experiences, rate your guide, and discover the UAE authentically.
